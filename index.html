Semua nama, logo, lambang skuad, laporan projek dan gambar di laman ini adalah hak milik Persatuan Belia Cendekia Holistik. Penggunaan tanpa kebenaran bertulis rasmi adalah dilarang sama sekali, termasuk penyalinan, pengubahsuaian, penyiaran semula, atau penggunaan bagi mewakili mana-mana pihak, kutipan dana atau program lain.

Children and beneficiaries are protected

Faces of children and beneficiaries are masked or withheld throughout this site, in line with Akta Kanak-Kanak 2001. Volunteer contact details, emergency numbers, medical records, signatures and financial receipts are held in our internal records and are never published here. Do not attempt to identify, contact or photograph any person shown on this site.

About the figures

Impact numbers on this site are rounded down to the nearest sensible figure. Exact line-by-line totals sit in the internal National Impact Report and are released to sponsors and partners on request, not published openly.

Your data

If you register, the details you submit go to our organiser spreadsheet and are used only to place you in a squad and contact you about this programme. Under-18 applicants need a guardian's consent. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared beyond the committee and the platforms named in the registration form.

PRIVACY NOTICE

Your details are seen by the committee members running the intake. Where a programme is delivered with a partner or needs approval from a government agency, we share only what that requires, and only once the agreement with that partner is signed.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must consent in writing before you are placed on a programme.

We keep your record while you are an active volunteer and for two years after. You may ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by writing to us.

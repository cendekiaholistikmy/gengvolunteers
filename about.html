WHO WE ARE

NO COLLABORATION WITHOUT A SIGNED DOCUMENT

Every partnership rests on an MOU, an LOI or an official letter. Partner names appear on this page only after signing.

MONEY IS SEPARATED AND REPORTED

Cash, in kind contributions and surplus are reported as three separate figures, never merged.

CHILDREN ARE PROTECTED ON SITE

No photography of beneficiaries without confirmed consent. Images of children are published only with written permission from the guardian and the school. We never collect or publish location data of children.

PRIVACY NOTICE

Your details are seen by the committee members running the intake. Where a programme is delivered with a partner or needs approval from a government agency, we share only what that requires, and only once the agreement with that partner is signed.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must consent in writing before you are placed on a programme.

We keep your record while you are an active volunteer and for two years after. You may ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by writing to us.
